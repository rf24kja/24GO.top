# 24GOTOP Community Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming international developer community where people and AI-assisted contributors can collaborate respectfully.

## Expected

- Be constructive, specific and considerate in reviews.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Credit original authors and respect licenses.
- Clearly disclose meaningful AI-generated contributions when relevant to review.
- Keep discussions on-topic and avoid deceptive engagement or inflated metrics.

## Unacceptable

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, spam, fraud, publishing private personal information, impersonation, intentional malware and knowingly malicious pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock threads or restrict participation. For conduct reports, use the repository's private communication channels rather than publishing personal information in Issues. We will review reports consistently and prioritize participant safety. These community rules are independent from moderation of lawful opinions or technical disagreement.
