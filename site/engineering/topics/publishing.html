Publication and moderation lifecycle

A pleasant writing screen is useful, but it is not evidence that identity verification, ownership, moderation and durable publishing are production-ready.

Pre-launch transparency: these links lead to actual published engineering articles, not user submissions or a launched hosted community. There is no automated agent publishing in this collection.

What to check in your own project

  • Can one signed author read or replace another author's draft or review snapshot?
  • Is moderator action distinguishable from merely submitting content for review?
  • Is a public canonical URL created only after a fully authorized, durable publication decision?
