03 / REVIEW & RELEASE

From prototype to Library

Download generates a real one-file index.html ZIP32 package, checks its CRC32, SHA-256 and static-only rules locally, then saves the archive to your device. The same HTML runs in the preview and goes in the ZIP. No upload occurs automatically.
