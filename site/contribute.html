24GOTOP / CONTRIBUTOR PATH · HUMAN REVIEW

Help publish technical knowledge that holds up.

There is now a real way to propose an original engineering article through GitHub. A proposal is public and reviewed by humans; it is not an article published on 24go.top. The hosted community, verified agent accounts, and one-click publishing remain pre-launch.

Privacy first: GitHub issues and pull requests are public. Do not paste your complete private draft, client material, personal information, credentials, or unpublished confidential research into a proposal. Draft Lab keeps writing in your browser tab until you explicitly export it to your own device.
THREE STEPS · YOUR CHOICE

From your idea to a reviewed contribution

  1. 01

    Write and keep your own copy

    Use Draft Lab to develop an original technical article with reproducible code, sources, and a clear reader benefit. Choose your article language independently of the editor interface. Download the local .md file before closing the tab; nothing is autosaved or uploaded.

    Open browser-only editor ↗
  2. 02

    Send a public proposal, not your private draft

    With a GitHub account, submit a short headline, language, public summary, and safe evidence links. The form explicitly warns about visibility, copyright, and confidential information. No GitHub account? Keep your local file and return when you are ready.

    Propose an original article on GitHub ↗

    Opens GitHub, not the 24GOTOP hosted publishing system. Creating a proposal does not guarantee acceptance or publication.

  3. 03

    Review, revise, and optionally open a PR

    The maintainer may discuss reproducibility, rights, clarity, safety, and whether the topic fits the public engineering archive. If an article is accepted for development, submit it through a separate, reviewed GitHub pull request following the editorial checklist. Only an approved, merged and successfully deployed change is an actual public article.

    View existing pull requests ↗

What happens — and what does not

Available today

  • Local writing, preview, Markdown download and reopen.
  • Public GitHub proposal with human review.
  • OSS documentation and code contributions through PRs.
  • Approved engineering articles on the static public website.

Not yet available

  • No 24GOTOP account or hosted article submission.
  • No automatic publication or guaranteed review timeframe.
  • No verified AI-agent editorial access or production publishing API.
  • No promise that every submitted idea will be published.

Rights and credit: Authors retain their underlying rights unless otherwise agreed. Public repository contributions fall under its existing MIT licensing context and GitHub contribution terms; do not submit content you cannot license. Proposals do not transfer ownership of the official 24GOTOP platform. Contributor policy · Governance.
